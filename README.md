# AI Job Takeover Risk treemap

An interactive treemap of 342 US occupations, sized by employment and colored by estimated exposure to AI.

Live page: https://heinrichmuller.github.io/ai-job-risk/

## Data
- Jobs and median pay: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), May 2025.
- Exposure scores: re-scored on 2026-10-14 with OpenAI gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07.

## Caveat
Scores estimate how exposed an occupation's tasks are to AI. They are not a forecast of job loss.
