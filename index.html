About this map

Data: BLS OEWS May 2025 national employment and median annual wages for 341 occupations.

AI exposure scores (0 to 10 per occupation) were re-scored on 14 Oct 2026 with OpenAI gpt-5-mini (2025-08-07), using the original project's scoring prompt.

Headline figures: 132.6M jobs covered; job-weighted average exposure 5.06; $3.59T in annual wages sit in occupations scored 7 or higher (of $8.61T total).

Caveats: the model never scored any occupation below 2, so the "minimal" band is empty. Scores are model estimates of exposure to AI, not predictions of job loss.

Credit: built on mariodian/jobs, a fork of Andrej Karpathy's original.
